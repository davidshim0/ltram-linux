WARM is the topology: a knee is where WARM_STEP jumps and then settles.

COLD is a DRAM latency only while the working set spans enough pages that
the prefetcher cannot amortise the fill. Below roughly 1 MiB a cold pass
touches few enough pages that hardware prefetch fills them wholesale, and
COLD becomes a bandwidth figure -- do not read the small-size COLD numbers
as latencies. Its diagnostic value is at the top of the sweep: if COLD
there is not flat near the DRAM latency, the scrub is not evicting and
nothing below can be trusted either.
